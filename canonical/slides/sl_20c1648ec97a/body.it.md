* Massimo $1$ ovvero $\qty{100}{\percent}$

---
#### Procedimento
* dati: $U = \qty{12,5}{\volt}$
* dati: $I = \qty{16}{\ampere}$
* dati: $P_\text{ab} = \qty{90}{\watt}$
* incognita: $\eta$

<fragment>
$P_\text{zu} = U \cdot I = \qty{12,5}{\volt} \cdot \qty{16}{\ampere} = \qty{200}{\watt}$
</fragment>
<fragment>
$\eta = \frac{P_\text{ab}}{P_\text{zu}} = \frac{\qty{90}{\watt}}{\qty{200}{\watt}} = \qty{45}{\percent}$
</fragment>
