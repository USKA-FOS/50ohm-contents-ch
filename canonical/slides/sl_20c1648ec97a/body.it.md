* Massimo $1$ o $\qty{100}{\percent}$

#### Percorso di soluzione
* dato: $U = \qty{12,5}{\volt}$
* dato: $I = \qty{16}{\ampere}$
* dato: $P_\text{out} = \qty{90}{\watt}$
* cercato: $\eta$

<fragment>
$P_\text{in} = U \cdot I = \qty{12,5}{\volt} \cdot \qty{16}{\ampere} = \qty{200}{\watt}$
</fragment>
<fragment>
$\eta = \frac{P_\text{out}}{P_\text{in}} = \frac{\qty{90}{\watt}}{\qty{200}{\watt}} = \qty{45}{\percent}$
</fragment>
